# Cortex Harness

Cortex Harness is the open protocol for CORTEX work. Blocks are the public
units. The CORTEX Kernel remains the authority for leases, replay, receipts,
and completion decisions.

The public constitution is the [CORTEX Six Laws](./constitution), also called
the CORTEX Collaboration Constitution.

These pages are the source for <https://cortexharness.org>. English is the
source language. The Chinese pages are the display translation.
